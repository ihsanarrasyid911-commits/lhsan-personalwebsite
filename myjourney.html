MY JOURNEY

"Every Great Journey Begins Somewhere"

My journey as a creator began with curiosity. At first, I was simply someone who used technology and enjoyed exploring what it could do. Like many other people, I used applications, visited websites, and experienced the incredible things that technology could offer. However, as time passed, my curiosity grew stronger. I started asking myself how these things were created, how they worked behind the scenes, and how I could eventually build something of my own. I realized that there was an entire world behind every application, website, and digital experience I encountered. That curiosity slowly became the beginning of a new journey. Instead of remaining someone who only used technology, I wanted to understand how to create it. I wanted to discover the ideas, logic, and creativity that transformed simple lines of code into something useful. This realization gave me a new perspective. Technology was no longer just something I enjoyed using; it became something I wanted to understand, explore, and contribute to in my own way. Learning to code has become one of the most meaningful experiences in my life. My journey began with Python, a programming language that introduced me to the world of programming logic. At first, many things seemed unfamiliar. Variables, conditions, loops, and other programming concepts challenged the way I thought.

Sometimes, I could understand an example but still struggle to create my own solution. There were moments when I needed to read an explanation several times before the idea finally made sense. Nevertheless, every small discovery encouraged me to keep going. Python taught me that programming is not simply about writing commands and expecting a computer to do something. It requires logical thinking, patience, and the ability to break a complicated problem into smaller and more manageable parts. I began to understand that every instruction has a purpose and that even a small symbol can influence how a program behaves. Gradually, I learned to pay more attention to the reasons behind the code rather than focusing only on the final result. After exploring Python, I became interested in HTML and CSS. These technologies introduced me to another side of creating things with code. Through HTML, I learned how to build the structure of a website, organize information, add headings and paragraphs, insert images, and connect different pages. Through CSS, I discovered how to customize the appearance of those pages using colors, borders, spacing, shadows, fonts, and other visual properties. It was exciting to realize that I could turn my ideas into a website that I could actually see and interact with. Creating my personal website has become an important milestone in this journey. Although it may look like a simple project, it represents something meaningful to me. It is one of my first opportunities to combine what I have learned with my own ideas. Every page gives me a chance to experiment with layouts, organize my thoughts, and discover new ways to express myself through technology. More importantly, this project reminds me that I do not have to wait until I become an expert to start creating something of my own. Of course, the process is not always smooth.
