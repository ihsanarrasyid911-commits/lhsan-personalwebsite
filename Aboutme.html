"ABOUT ME"

Hello! My name is Ihsan, and welcome to my personal website! I am a student who has a strong curiosity about technology, computers, programming, and the digital world. I believe that technology is more than just a tool for everyday life. It is also a way to express creativity, explore new ideas, solve problems, and turn imagination into something real. I am someone who enjoys learning new things and discovering how the world works. I know that there are still many things I do not understand, but that is exactly what makes learning interesting to me. Every new thing I learn gives me a reason to keep exploring and improving myself. My journey into programming started with curiosity. At first, coding looked unfamiliar and complicated to me. There were many symbols, commands, and lines of code that I did not understand. However, instead of giving up, I decided to take my first steps and learn everything gradually, one concept at a time. Currently, I am learning HTML, CSS, and Python. Through HTML, I am learning how to build the structure of a website. Through CSS, I am discovering how to design and customize the appearance of web pages. Meanwhile, Python is helping me understand programming logic and how to give instructions to a computer. Every small project I create becomes part of my learning journey. Sometimes, I make mistakes, encounter unexpected problems, or spend time trying to understand why my code does not work as expected. However, I believe that mistakes are a natural part of the learning process. They help me understand things more deeply and encourage me to become more patient, careful, and persistent. For me, becoming a programmer is not simply about memorizing code or learning different programming languages. It is about developing the ability to think logically, solve problems, understand how things work, and create something useful through technology.
